# Publication route

## Recommended route
1. Complete the experiment and write the results with no fabricated values.
2. Get a faculty mentor or researcher to review the contribution and methods.
3. For a strong peer-reviewed target, investigate SIGIR 2027 and other suitable IR venues. Check official calls and deadlines at submission time. The SIGIR 2027 page currently labels some dates proposed and says the full paper requires a prior abstract; check it again before planning submission.
4. If you want a public preprint, arXiv may be an option after checking its submission rules, category fit, endorsement requirements, and license. A preprint is not peer review and does not mean acceptance.
5. Follow the venue's rules about prior preprints, anonymity, AI use, authorship, and simultaneous submissions. Never submit the same paper to multiple venues at the same time if their rules prohibit it.

## Official links
- SIGIR 2027 full-paper call: https://sigir2027.org/pages/submit-full.html
- ECIR 2027 full-paper call: https://www.ecir2027.co.uk/call-for-full-papers
- arXiv submission help: https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html

## Current caution
The ECIR 2027 full-paper dates listed on its website are 21 September 2026 for abstract and 5 October 2026 for full submission; as of 17 October 2026 those dates have passed. Do not assume a late submission is possible. SIGIR 2027 lists proposed abstract and full-paper dates of 14 January 2027 and 21 January 2027 respectively; verify official updates before relying on them.

Do not upload this draft as if it were a finished paper. Fill every placeholder, run and document experiments, verify citations, confirm novelty, and ensure all authors approve the submission.
